CONTROLS & RULES

        
HOW TO PLAY

        

          
ADMoveExplore the world horizontally.

          
SPACEJump / Double JumpPress SPACE again in the air for one extra jump. Use it to clear shockwaves and reach high platforms.

          
SHIFTDash / DodgeBurst left or right with brief invulnerability. Dash through a boss charge or escape contact range.

          
SFast-Fall / DropHold S in the air to descend quickly. Press S while standing on a battle platform to drop through it.

          
JAttackUse your currently equipped weapon against corrupt creatures.

          
EInteractUse shrines, terminals, chests, and portals.

          
1234HotbarWeapon, Concept Power, torch, guide.

          
QCycle WeaponsSwitch between weapons you have collected from enemy and boss drops.

          
2KConcept PowerChoose from 21 skills in the Field Guide (C or slot 4). Your explorer starts with one random skill. Press 2 then click, or press K to cast instantly while keeping your current weapon equipped. It upgrades when you defeat mobs and bosses.

          
MOUSEUse selected slotWeapon attacks, Concept Power casts, and Torches place with left click.

          
CGuideOpen the Python field guide for the current world.

          
GPSGold BeaconThe right-side Mission GPS and gold world beacon always point to the next required objective.

          
ESCPause / closeClose menus. Save file manually before leaving.

        

        

          
          
          
        

        
D-pad: move; up: jump; down: drop. A: jump (tap twice for a double jump). Hold B to attack. L: dash. R: power. Use: talk or open a shrine. Select: change weapon. Start: pause, save or adjust sound. During a mob question, use ▲/▼ or tap an answer, then Confirm. The fight stays paused.

        
        
Boss strategy: use the arena platforms for high ground and projectile cover. Dash through telegraphed charges, double-jump over ground shockwaves, fast-fall to change direction, and choose melee or ranged weapons based on your position.

        
Play at your own pace. Coding challenges open at shrines and terminals. Close a challenge to return to your explorer.
